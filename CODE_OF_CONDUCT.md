# Community code of conduct

Frontier contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces
welcoming, technical, and respectful.

Examples of constructive participation include giving actionable feedback,
explaining assumptions, accepting correction, and focusing disagreement on
code, evidence, and modeling contracts. Harassment, personal attacks,
discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, and publication of another
person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns
privately to the maintainers listed in `README.md`. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, and retaliation against reporters is prohibited.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, project chat,
and public events where someone is representing Frontier.
